## AI and tool use

OpenAI Codex assisted with handbook and repository review, structural data
audits, documentation, Colab workflow preparation, and code updates. It
proposed the two label mappings that the team and independent Kiswahili
reviewer accepted, and generated slot types, values, and character offsets for
26 team-authored requests. The request text itself was authored by the team.
Codex also supported the frontend's semantic-frame display and added tests for
response frames, clarification choices, typo normalization, and date
formatting.

An independent Kiswahili reviewer approved the text, labels, slot annotations,
and grouping for all 250 records. Contributor and reviewer codes are
pseudonyms; the identity key and permission evidence are retained privately.
The structural audit checks schema, splits, provenance fields, and character
spans. Human review covers linguistic quality and annotation decisions.
